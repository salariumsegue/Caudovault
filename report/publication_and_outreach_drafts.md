## 1. Preprint Abstract Draft

**Title:** Discovery of Divergent Tailed Bacteriophages and Complete Orphan Viral Lineages in an Assembled Freshwater Metagenome

**Abstract:**
Freshwater ecosystems harbor vast uncharacterized viral diversity, yet standard nucleotide k-mer screening often fails to resolve evolutionary relationships for divergent bacteriophages. Using a reproducible hallmark-guided computational screen of 221,228 assembled contigs from freshwater metagenome `ERZ18661299`, we recovered 93 candidate tailed dsDNA phage (*Caudoviricetes*) genomes lacking close nucleotide matches in NCBI Viral RefSeq (`<= 1.55%` sequence coverage) or surveyed freshwater viromes (`<= 3.10%`). Whole-proteome phylogenetic placement of seven priority contigs (`34.2–77.6 kb`) using ViPTree v4.2 against 7,097 prokaryotic dsDNA reference viruses resolved five novel genus- to subfamily-level lineages exceeding the $S_G > 0.02$ proteomic similarity threshold, including a Mu-like transposable *Pseudomonadota* phage (`CTG_000296`, $S_G = 0.2290$ to *Ralstonia* phage YO374_1), a high-GC *Actinomycetota*/cyanophage-allied genome (`CTG_000058`, $S_G = 0.0865$), two distantly related *Pseudomonadota* phages (`CTG_000169` and `CTG_000137`, sharing cross-contig $S_G = 0.0427$), and a complete direct-terminal-repeat (DTR)-closed phage (`CTG_000273`, `37,077 bp`, $S_G = 0.0438$). Crucially, two priority contigs fell entirely below ViPTree's $S_G = 0.02$ relatedness cutoff: `CTG_000145` (`51,844 bp`, max $S_G = 0.0049$) and the complete, DTR-closed `CTG_000311` (`34,177 bp`, `55 bp` DTR, 51 ORFs, max $S_G = 0.0174$), which encodes a complete head-packaging-tail-lysis architecture alongside a queuosine-like tRNA/DNA modification cassette (*QueE*, GTP cyclohydrolase, queuine tRNA-ribosyltransferase). Follow-up NCBI `ClusteredNR` BLASTp analysis (`RID: FUJZE5Z7014`) of all 11 hallmark proteins resolved both ViPTree orphans to uncultured metagenome-assembled (MAG) viral clades absent from cultured RefSeq databases: `CTG_000311` shares syntenic HK97-like structural loci (`33–56%` aa identity) with uncultured Candidate Phyla Radiation (*Patescibacteria*: *Candidatus Moraniibacteriota* and *Parcubacteria*) phages, whereas `CTG_000145` aligns with an uncultured Mu-like (`gpT`) environmental MAG prophage clade (`46–69%` aa identity across *Saccharimonadales*, *Sulfuriferula*, and *Bacteroidia*). These results demonstrate that combining hallmark HMM screening, whole-proteome trees, and `ClusteredNR` searches uncovers complete, deeply divergent freshwater phage lineages—including CPR-associated HK97-like and uncultured Mu-like clades—that remain invisible to nucleotide-only classifiers and cultured-phage trees.

## 3. LinkedIn Post Draft

We just finished whole-proteome phylogenetic screening, NCBI `ClusteredNR` follow-up, and raw-read DTR junction validation on 7 priority freshwater bacteriophage genomes recovered from metagenome `ERZ18661299`—and the results highlight just how much viral dark matter nucleotide classifiers and cultured-phage databases miss.

At the DNA k-mer level, all 7 genomes showed 0.0% whole-genome similarity to NCBI Viral RefSeq. Using ViPTree v4.2 across 7,097 reference dsDNA phages plus `ClusteredNR` BLASTp (`RID: FUJZE5Z7014`), we found:

- **5 novel genus/subfamily-level lineages** spanning Mu-like *Pseudomonadota* phages (`CTG_000296`, max $S_G = 0.2290$), aquatic *Gammaproteobacteria* phages (`CTG_000169`, `CTG_000137`), high-GC *Actinomycetota*/cyanophage-allied phages (`CTG_000058`), and a complete DTR-closed genome (`CTG_000273`, 37.1 kb, `29.7x` depth, `116` full-DTR spanning reads).
- **2 ViPTree "orphan" lineages resolved to uncultured environmental MAG clades:** `CTG_000145` (ViPTree $S_G = 0.0049$) resolves to an uncultured Mu-like (`gpT`) MAG prophage clade (`46–69%` aa id), while **`CTG_000311`**—a complete, DTR-closed 34.2 kb genome (ViPTree $S_G = 0.0174$, `15.5x` depth, `100%` coverage with continuous DTR seam-spanning reads) carrying a full structural/lysis suite plus a 3-gene queuosine/7-deazapurine modification module—shows syntenic homology (`33–56%` aa id) to uncultured CPR (*Patescibacteria*: *Moraniibacteriota* / *Parcubacteria*) HK97-like phages!
