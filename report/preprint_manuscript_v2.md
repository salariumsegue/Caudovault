# Identification and Read-Level Validation of Two Complete, Terminally Redundant Caudoviricetes Assemblies (`CTG_000273` and `CTG_000311`) from Lake Mekkojärvi

**Authors:** Niall L. Gillen¹*  
**Affiliations:** ¹Indiana University Bloomington, Bloomington, IN, USA  
**Correspondence:** ngillen@iu.edu  

## Abstract

Freshwater ecosystems harbor vast uncharacterized viral diversity, yet standard nucleotide k-mer screening often fails to resolve evolutionary relationships for divergent bacteriophages. Using a hallmark-guided computational screen of 221,228 assembled contigs from public Lake Mekkojärvi metagenome `ERZ18661299` (Evo region, Finland; BioProject `PRJEB47226`, BioSample `SAMEA6872951`, raw reads `ERR4194644`), we recovered 93 candidate tailed dsDNA phage (*Caudoviricetes*) contigs and focused full phylogenomic, database-wide, raw-read, and CRISPR-spacer validation on two CheckV-complete, direct-terminal-repeat (DTR)-supported assemblies: `CTG_000273` (`37,077 bp` linear / `37,043 bp` DTR-trimmed, `34 bp` DTR, `44.62%` GC, `48` linear / `47` joined ORFs) and `CTG_000311` (`34,177 bp` linear / `34,122 bp` DTR-trimmed, `55 bp` DTR, `37.44%` GC, `51` ORFs). Both assemblies exhibit `0.0%` aligned bases against frozen NCBI Viral RefSeq and surveyed freshwater virome snapshots; querying full NCBI `core_nt` (sensitive BLASTn, `RID: FVPFG1V3014`) for `CTG_000273` yielded at most `5%` query coverage (`63.71%` identity to *Polynucleobacter paneuropaeus* `CP049682.1`, $E = 2 \times 10^{-53}$; `4%` cover at `64.58%` to MAG phage `BK031899.1`), indicating genuine database-wide novelty with only weak sequence affinity (and `0` local CRISPR spacer matches across `5,165` spacers in `ERZ18661299`). `CTG_000311` fell below ViPTree's $S_G = 0.02$ cutoff ($S_G = 0.0174$ to *Parageobacillus* phage JGon-2020a `CP063417`), but full NCBI `ClusteredNR` BLASTp (`RID: FUJZE5Z7014`) revealed conserved synteny (`33.00%–55.65%` amino acid identity) across five consecutive structural/packaging proteins with uncultured Candidate Phyla Radiation (*Patescibacteria*: *Candidatus Moraniibacteriota* and *Parcubacteria*) MAGs alongside a three-gene queuosine/7-deazapurine cassette (*QueE*, GTP cyclohydrolase, queuine tRNA-ribosyltransferase), establishing `CTG_000311` as a novel genus-level genome within an uncultured CPR lineage. Local CRISPR-array screening further identified an exact `26/26 bp` spacer match (`0` mismatches) on `ERZ18661299.43738` (`1,040 bp`, 16 repeats of `36 bp`), linking `CTG_000311` to a co-occurring Lake Mekkojärvi host. Mapping `ERR4194644` reads across DTR-bridging references confirmed `100.00%` base coverage for both `CTG_000273` (`29.72x` mean depth; `116` full-DTR reads fusing terminal ORFs `_48` and `_1` in-frame into a `162-aa` DNA repair protein) and `CTG_000311` (`15.45x` mean depth; `8` left-seam, `9` right-seam, and `3` full-DTR reads providing preliminary, suggestive junction support). Physical virion circularity versus linear DTR packaging remains unresolved.

## 1. Introduction

Bacteriophages of the class *Caudoviricetes* drive microbial mortality, horizontal gene transfer, and biogeochemical cycling across freshwater ecosystems. Even protein-based viral trees built primarily from cultured isolates under-represent phages infecting uncultured bacterial phyla, notably the Candidate Phyla Radiation (CPR / *Patescibacteria*), whose ultra-small, largely uncultivated hosts are abundant in humic and oligotrophic freshwater lakes. Here, we apply a reproducible, multi-stage computational screen to public freshwater metagenome `ERZ18661299` from humic Lake Mekkojärvi (Evo region, Finland; BioProject `PRJEB47226`, BioSample `SAMEA6872951`, raw reads `ERR4194644`) and perform end-to-end functional annotation, whole-proteome placement, full NCBI `core_nt`/`ClusteredNR` novelty verification, raw-read DTR junction validation, and local CRISPR-spacer host screening on two complete, DTR-supported assemblies: `CTG_000273` and `CTG_000311`.

## 2. Methods

### 2.1 Metagenome Preprocessing and Hallmark-Guided Screening
Assembled contigs from ENA metagenome `ERZ18661299` (`221,228` raw records) were filtered by length (`>= 5 kb` retained, `n = 3,388`; priority tier `>= 10 kb`, `n = 1,502`; `< 5 kb` archived, `n = 217,810`; `30` corrupt/duplicate records excluded). Open reading frames were called with Prodigal v2.6.3 (genetic code 11) and scanned against PHROGs v4 profile HMMs. Contigs encoding at least two independent viral hallmark categories (`major_capsid`, `portal`, `terminase_large`) or one hallmark plus at least three supporting phage genes without cellular housekeeping contamination were retained (`n = 93`), and CheckV was used to identify complete, DTR-supported assemblies, selecting `CTG_000273` (`ERZ18661299.273`) and `CTG_000311` (`ERZ18661299.311`) for complete validation.

### 2.2 Novelty, Whole-Proteome Phylogenetics, and Database-Wide Checks
Nucleotide similarity was evaluated against frozen NCBI Viral RefSeq and surveyed freshwater virome snapshots (`0.0%` aligned bases for both `CTG_000273` and `CTG_000311`). Whole-proteome phylogenetic placement was performed with ViPTree v4.2 (session `u6lWCt_eU2Wt7KD86pfmNw`, Prodigal code 11) against `7,097` prokaryotic dsDNA reference viral genomes (`7,104` total entries). To test novelty beyond RefSeq, `CTG_000273` (`37,043 bp` DTR-trimmed) was queried against full NCBI `core_nt` using sensitive `blastn` (`RID: FVPFG1V3014`), and all six hallmark proteins of ViPTree-divergent `CTG_000311` ($S_G < 0.02$) were queried against full NCBI `ClusteredNR` using `blastp` (`RID: FUJZE5Z7014`).

### 2.3 Raw-Read DTR Junction Verification and Local CRISPR-Spacer Screening
Redundant 3' DTRs were trimmed from `CTG_000273` (`34 bp` -> `37,043 bp`) and `CTG_000311` (`55 bp` -> `34,122 bp`), and `2 kb` junction-bridging references (`1,000 bp` 3' flank + `1,000 bp` 5' flank, placing each DTR seam at position `1001`) were constructed (`results/priority_7/dtr_junction_refs.fna`). Paired-end raw reads from ENA run `ERR4194644` were mapped using `minimap2 -ax sr` (v2.31) and filtered with `samtools` (v1.25; primary alignments, `MAPQ >= 20`, `NM <= 3`). Reads spanning the left seam, right seam, and entire DTR with at least `15 bp` overhang on both flanks were quantified, and genome-wide coverage profiles were computed with `samtools depth -a`. For local host linking, all `47,265` contigs `>= 1 kb` in `ERZ18661299` were scanned for CRISPR arrays (`>= 3` identical `23–47 bp` direct repeats spaced `24–55 bp` apart), yielding `5,165` spacers across `86` CRISPR-array contigs, which were matched against both strands of `CTG_000273` and `CTG_000311` (`<= 2` mismatches).

### 3.1 Genome Architecture, Novelty, and Host Evidence
* **`CTG_000273`:** Encodes a complete head-packaging-tail-replication architecture (portal `phrog_1216`, major capsid `phrog_1328`, head scaffolding `phrog_3927`, tail `phrog_11262`, `TerS` `phrog_23444`, polymerase `phrog_2825`, DnaD-like helicase loader `phrog_2514`, RecT `phrog_148`, DNA methyltransferase `phrog_757`, SbcC-like endonuclease `phrog_77`). Against full NCBI `core_nt` (`RID: FVPFG1V3014`), sensitive `blastn` recovered at most `5%` query coverage (*Polynucleobacter paneuropaeus* `CP049682.1` at `63.71%` id, $E = 2 \times 10^{-53}$; MAG phage `BK031899.1` at `4%` cover, `64.58%` id; *Bordetella* phage BPP-1 `NC_005357.1` at `3%` cover, `65.16%` id), representing weak sequence affinity rather than a host assignment, and local CRISPR screening yielded `0` spacer hits. Joining across the `34 bp` DTR fuses linear ORFs `CTG_000273_48` and `CTG_000273_1` in-frame into a single complete `162-aa` DNA repair protein (`phrog_10276`), directly supported by `116` full-DTR reads.
* **`CTG_000311`:** Encodes a complete HK97-like virion morphogenesis and lysis suite (portal `phrog_2662`, prohead protease `phrog_7015`, major capsid `phrog_15928`, Neck1 `phrog_5`, tail tape measure `phrog_3981`, endolysin `phrog_291`, SSB `phrog_44`, NinB `phrog_72`, exonucleases `phrog_298`, `phrog_10477`) alongside a three-gene queuosine/7-deazapurine cassette (`phrog_887`, `phrog_2197`, `phrog_2076`). Sitting below ViPTree's $S_G = 0.02$ cutoff (`0.0174`), `CTG_000311`'s five consecutive structural/packaging proteins (`_16`, `_18`, `_21`, `_22`, `_23`) matched syntenic consecutive loci in uncultured *Candidatus Moraniibacteriota* (`HBI50266.1`–`HBI50277.1`, `33.00%–55.65%` aa id) and *Parcubacteria* (`MFA5777622.1`–`MFA5777624.1`, `42.37%–53.21%` aa id) MAGs in `ClusteredNR` (`RID: FUJZE5Z7014`), while `TerL` (`_24`) hit *Candidatus Altimarinota* (`MHY2402454.1`, `49.35%` id, $E = 10^{-149}$). Local CRISPR screening identified an exact `26/26 bp` spacer match (`CTAGCCAATTTTGACGATAATTCATT`, `0` mismatches) on `1,040 bp` CRISPR-array contig `ERZ18661299.43738` (`16` repeats of `36 bp`), linking `CTG_000311` to a co-occurring Lake Mekkojärvi host. Its `8` left-seam, `9` right-seam, and `3` full-DTR reads (`10x` min depth) provide preliminary, suggestive junction support.

## 4. Discussion and Data Availability

Together, these analyses establish `CTG_000273` as a genuinely novel, DTR-validated freshwater *Caudoviricetes* genome and `CTG_000311` as a novel genus-level member of an uncultured CPR (*Patescibacteria*) HK97-like lineage with direct local CRISPR-spacer evidence. Physical virion topology (circular vs. linear with DTRs) remains unresolved without long-read or physical topology validation.
