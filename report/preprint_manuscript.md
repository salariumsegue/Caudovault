# Identification and Read-Level Validation of Previously Uncharacterized Caudoviricetes Genomes and a Complete CPR-Associated Phage Lineage from Lake Mekkojarvi

**Authors:** Niall Gillen¹*

## Abstract

Freshwater ecosystems harbor vast uncharacterized viral diversity, yet standard nucleotide k-mer screening often fails to resolve evolutionary relationships for divergent bacteriophages. Using a reproducible hallmark-guided computational screen of 221,228 assembled contigs from public Lake Mekkojarvi metagenome `ERZ18661299` (BioProject `PRJEB47226`, BioSample `SAMEA6872951`, raw reads `ERR4194644`), we recovered 93 previously uncharacterized candidate tailed dsDNA phage (*Caudoviricetes*) genomes lacking close nucleotide matches in NCBI Viral RefSeq (`<= 1.55%` sequence coverage) or surveyed freshwater viromes (`<= 3.10%`). Whole-proteome phylogenetic placement of seven priority contigs (`34.2–77.6 kb`) using ViPTree v4.2 against 7,097 prokaryotic dsDNA reference viruses resolved five potentially novel genus- to subfamily-level lineages exceeding the $S_G > 0.02$ proteomic similarity threshold, including a Mu-like transposable *Pseudomonadota* phage (`CTG_000296`, $S_G = 0.2290$ to *Ralstonia* phage YO374_1), a high-GC *Actinomycetota*/cyanophage-allied genome (`CTG_000058`, $S_G = 0.0865$), two distantly related *Pseudomonadota* phages (`CTG_000169` and `CTG_000137`, sharing cross-contig $S_G = 0.0427$), and a direct-terminal-repeat (DTR)-supported phage (`CTG_000273`, `37,077 bp` linear / `37,043 bp` DTR-trimmed, $S_G = 0.0438$ to *Bordetella* phage BPP-1 `NC_005357`, `0.0%` nucleotide alignment to nearest k-mer anchor `NC_049436`). Two priority contigs fell below ViPTree's $S_G = 0.02$ relatedness cutoff: `CTG_000145` (`51,844 bp`, max $S_G = 0.0049$) and DTR-supported `CTG_000311` (`34,177 bp` linear / `34,122 bp` DTR-trimmed, `55 bp` DTR, 51 ORFs, max $S_G = 0.0174$ to *Parageobacillus* phage JGon-2020a `CP063417`, `0.0%` nucleotide alignment to `NC_070936`), which encodes a complete HK97-like head-packaging-tail-lysis architecture alongside a three-gene queuosine/7-deazapurine modification cassette (*QueE*, GTP cyclohydrolase, queuine tRNA-ribosyltransferase). Follow-up NCBI `ClusteredNR` BLASTp analysis (`RID: FUJZE5Z7014`) resolved both ViPTree orphans to uncultured metagenome-assembled (MAG) viral clades absent from cultured RefSeq databases: `CTG_000311` shares syntenic structural loci (`33%–56%` amino acid identity) with uncultured Candidate Phyla Radiation (*Patescibacteria*: *Candidatus Moraniibacteriota* and *Parcubacteria*) phages, whereas `CTG_000145` aligns with an uncultured Mu-like (`gpT`) environmental MAG prophage clade (`46%–69%` aa identity across *Saccharimonadales*, *Sulfuriferula*, and *Bacteroidia*). Finally, mapping raw paired-end reads (`ERR4194644`) across DTR-bridging references confirmed `100.00%` genome coverage and continuous seam-spanning read support for both `CTG_000273` (`29.72x` mean depth, `116` full-DTR reads, fusing terminal ORFs `_48` and `_1` in-frame) and `CTG_000311` (`15.45x` mean depth, `8` left-seam, `9` right-seam, and `3` full-DTR reads). These results support both as terminally redundant, DTR-joined assemblies (with physical virion circularity vs. linear DTR packaging left unresolved) and highlight previously uncharacterized freshwater phage lineages missed by nucleotide-only classifiers.

## 1. Introduction

Bacteriophages of the class *Caudoviricetes* drive microbial mortality, horizontal gene transfer, and biogeochemical cycling across freshwater ecosystems. Despite rapid growth in viral metagenomics, a large fraction of assembled freshwater viral contigs—often termed "viral dark matter"—cannot be classified by standard nucleotide k-mer or alignment tools because dsDNA phage genomes accumulate rapid synonymous and structural divergence across distant lineages. Even protein-based viral trees built primarily from cultured isolates (such as RefSeq-anchored proteomic trees) under-represent phages infecting uncultured bacterial phyla, notably the Candidate Phyla Radiation (CPR / *Patescibacteria*), whose ultra-small, largely uncultivated hosts are abundant in humic and oligotrophic freshwater lakes.

Here, we apply a reproducible, multi-stage computational screen to public freshwater metagenome `ERZ18661299` from humic Lake Mekkojarvi (BioProject `PRJEB47226`, BioSample `SAMEA6872951`, raw reads `ERR4194644`). By combining PHROGs profile HMM hallmark detection, CheckV completeness/DTR profiling, ViPTree v4.2 whole-proteome phylogenomics, NCBI `ClusteredNR` homology searches, and raw-read DTR junction verification, we recover 93 previously uncharacterized *Caudoviricetes* candidates and characterize two terminally redundant, DTR-joined assemblies (`CTG_000273` and `CTG_000311`).

## 2. Methods

### 2.1 Metagenome Preprocessing and Hallmark-Guided Screening
Assembled contigs from ENA metagenome `ERZ18661299` (`221,228` raw records) were filtered by length (`>= 5 kb` retained, `n = 3,388`; priority tier `>= 10 kb`, `n = 1,502`; `< 5 kb` archived, `n = 217,810`; `30` corrupt/duplicate records excluded). Open reading frames were called with Prodigal v2.6.3 (genetic code 11) and scanned against PHROGs v4 profile HMMs. Contigs were retained as candidate *Caudoviricetes* if they encoded at least two independent viral hallmark categories (`major_capsid`, `portal`, `terminase_large`) or one hallmark plus at least three supporting phage-annotated genes without cellular housekeeping contamination, yielding `93` candidates.

### 2.2 Nucleotide Novelty, Quality Assessment, and Priority Selection
Nucleotide similarity was evaluated against NCBI Viral RefSeq (`<= 1.55%` max shorter-sequence coverage), surveyed freshwater viromes (`<= 3.10%`), and within-dataset candidates (`<= 34.43%`), well below the MIUViG `85%` coverage / `95%` ANI species threshold. Specifically, `CTG_000273` (`ERZ18661299.273`) and `CTG_000311` (`ERZ18661299.311`) showed `0.0%` aligned bases (`0.0` aligned fraction) against their nearest k-mer anchors (`NC_049436` and `NC_070936`, respectively). Viral completeness and terminal repeats were assessed with CheckV (`5` Complete DTR-supported, `6` High-quality, `15` Medium-quality, `67` Low-quality, `0` Not-determined). Seven priority contigs (`34.2–77.6 kb`: `CTG_000058`, `CTG_000137`, `CTG_000145`, `CTG_000169`, `CTG_000273`, `CTG_000296`, and `CTG_000311`) were selected for deep phylogenomic and functional characterization.

### 2.3 Whole-Proteome Phylogenetics and NCBI `ClusteredNR` Resolution
The seven priority genomes were placed on a proteomic tree using ViPTree v4.2 (session `u6lWCt_eU2Wt7KD86pfmNw`, Prodigal code 11) against `7,097` prokaryotic dsDNA reference viral genomes (`7,104` total entries). Contigs with maximum proteomic similarity $S_G < 0.02$ (`CTG_000145` and `CTG_000311`) were designated ViPTree orphans, and all 11 of their hallmark proteins were queried against NCBI `ClusteredNR` via BLASTp (`RID: FUJZE5Z7014`) to detect uncultured environmental and MAG homologs.

### 2.4 Raw-Read DTR Junction Verification
For the two DTR-complete priority phages (`CTG_000273`, `34 bp` DTR; `CTG_000311`, `55 bp` DTR), the redundant 3' DTR was trimmed to yield terminally redundant assembly representations (`37,043 bp` and `34,122 bp`), and `2 kb` junction-bridging references (`1,000 bp` 3' flank + `1,000 bp` 5' flank, placing each DTR seam at position `1001`) were constructed (`results/priority_7/dtr_junction_refs.fna`). Paired-end raw reads from ENA run `ERR4194644` (`2.44 GB` + `2.55 GB`) were mapped using `minimap2 -ax sr` (v2.31) and filtered with `samtools` (v1.25; primary alignments, `MAPQ >= 20`, `NM <= 3`). Reads spanning the left seam, right seam, and entire DTR with at least `15 bp` overhang on both sides were quantified, and genome-wide coverage profiles were computed with `samtools depth -a`.

## 3. Results

### 3.1 Recovery of 93 Divergent Phage Candidates and Priority Cohort
All 93 *Caudoviricetes* candidates (`5.2–88.9 kb`) exhibited negligible nucleotide coverage (`<= 1.55%`) against NCBI Viral RefSeq, confirming that standard k-mer/ANI classifiers fail to assign them to known species. CheckV confirmed 100% completeness for five DTR-supported assemblies, including `CTG_000273` (`37,077 bp` linear / `37,043 bp` trimmed, `44.62%` GC, high-confidence `34 bp` DTR) and `CTG_000311` (`34,177 bp` linear / `34,122 bp` trimmed, `37.44%` GC, medium-confidence `55 bp` DTR).

### 3.3 `ClusteredNR` Links ViPTree Orphans to Uncultured CPR and Mu-like Clades
Querying all 11 hallmark proteins from `CTG_000145` (5 queries) and `CTG_000311` (6 queries) against NCBI `ClusteredNR` (`RID: FUJZE5Z7014`) revealed that both ViPTree orphans belong to uncultured environmental clades absent from cultured RefSeq trees:

* **`CTG_000311` (CPR / *Patescibacteria* HK97-like phage):** Five consecutive structural and packaging genes (`_16` HK97-gp10, `_18` head-tail connector, `_21` MCP, `_22` prohead protease, `_23` portal) match syntenic consecutive loci in uncultured *Candidatus Moraniibacteriota* (`HBI50266.1`–`HBI50277.1`, `33.00%–55.65%` aa identity) and *Parcubacteria* (`MFA5777622.1`–`MFA5777624.1`, `42.37%–53.21%` aa identity) MAGs, while `TerL` (`_24`) hits *Candidatus Altimarinota* (`MHY2402454.1`, `49.35%` id, $E = 10^{-149}$). Genome mapping (`results/figures/fig2_genome_map_CTG_000311.png`) further reveals a complete three-gene queuosine/7-deazapurine modification cassette (`phrog_887`, `phrog_2197`, `phrog_2076`) adjacent to the packaging module.
* **`CTG_000145` (Uncultured Mu-like `gpT` MAG prophage):** `CTG_000145_42` matches a Mu-like prophage major head subunit `gpT` (`HEY1645038.1`, `66.88%` aa identity, `100%` coverage, $E = 7 \times 10^{-155}$) from *Saccharimonadales*, with `TerL` (`_31`, `58.07%` id, $E = 0.0$), portal (`_37`, `46.27%` id, $E = 0.0$), and scaffolding (`_34`, `69.30%` id) matching the same *Saccharimonadales*/*Sulfuriferula*/*Bacteroidia*/*Occallatibacter* environmental prophage cluster.

### 3.4 Raw-Read Mapping Confirms Terminally Redundant Coverage Across DTR Seams
Mapping `ERR4194644` paired-end reads against the trimmed assemblies and DTR-bridging junction references (`results/figures/fig3_dtr_junction_coverage.png`) confirmed `100.00%` base coverage with zero dropouts for both assemblies:

* **`CTG_000273` (`37,043 bp` trimmed, `34 bp` DTR):** `7,478` mapped reads (`MAPQ >= 20`), `29.72x` mean depth (`29.0x` median), `23x` minimum seam depth, `157` left-seam, `150` right-seam, and `116` full-DTR spanning reads. Joining across the DTR seam merges linear ORFs `CTG_000273_48` and `CTG_000273_1` in-frame into a single complete `162-aa` DNA repair protein (`phrog_10276`).
* **`CTG_000311` (`34,122 bp` trimmed, `55 bp` DTR):** `3,541` mapped reads (`MAPQ >= 20`), `15.45x` mean depth (`15.0x` median), `10x` minimum seam depth, `8` left-seam, `9` right-seam, and `3` full-DTR spanning reads.

## 4. Discussion

Our findings highlight two complementary blind spots in routine viromics workflows. First, nucleotide k-mer and ANI classifiers failed to detect any close relatives (`<= 1.55%` coverage) among all 93 freshwater phage candidates, even for `CTG_000296`, which shares `22.90%` proteome similarity ($S_G = 0.2290$) with *Ralstonia* phages. Second, cultured-phage proteomic trees alone misclassify phages of uncultured bacterial phyla as isolated orphans: `CTG_000311` sits below ViPTree's $S_G = 0.02$ cutoff (`0.0174`) solely because CPR/*Patescibacteria* phages are absent from RefSeq, whereas `ClusteredNR` reveals conserved synteny across *Moraniibacteriota* and *Parcubacteria* MAGs. While short-read DTR-seam mapping confirms terminally redundant assembly continuity for both `CTG_000273` (`37,043 bp`) and `CTG_000311` (`34,122 bp`), physical virion topology (circular vs. linear with direct terminal repeats) remains unresolved without long-read or exonuclease/PFGE validation.
